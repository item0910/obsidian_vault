### 四条金科玉律

- 链接: [Four golden lessons | Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/426389a)

1. 不必知道所有事 -> 有规划的学习
	You don't have to know everything. And no one can.
2. 去深水，去沼泽
	where the action is.
3. 原谅浪费的时间
	get used to not being creative
4. 了解历史，让你的工作显得有价值 -> 找到人类的进步点
	learn the history
